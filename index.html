Cold weather work

Michigan winter is hard on a house.

Pipes freeze. Furnaces run non-stop and push a season of dust through every room. Salt and slush come in on everybody's boots. Here's what we get called for once the temperature drops.

01

Frozen and burst pipes

A pipe lets go behind a wall and a finished basement is under water in an hour. We extract, dry the structure with commercial air movers, treat for mold, and bill your insurance company direct.

24-hour response

02

Furnace-season air ducts

Your furnace is about to run for five months straight. Everything sitting in those ducts goes through the house with it. Cleaning them before the season is the cheapest air-quality fix there is.

Best done Oct–Dec

03

Holiday-ready carpets

Company coming. Book before Thanksgiving and we'll have the traffic lanes, the stairs and the dining room looking right — dry in hours, not days, so you're not working around it.

Books up fast in November

04

Salt, slush and grit

Road salt tracked into carpet grinds fibers down and leaves white bloom behind. Hot-water extraction pulls it out before it does permanent damage to your flooring.

Common questions

Things people ask before they call.

Do you clean carpets in Grand Rapids?

Yes. We're based in Allendale and run Grand Rapids, Wyoming, Kentwood, Grandville, Jenison, Hudsonville, Zeeland, Holland, Coopersville and Grand Haven every day. Water damage calls we take anywhere in the metro, any hour.

How long does carpet take to dry?

Usually a few hours. Truck-mounted hot water extraction recovers far more water than a rental machine, so the carpet is damp rather than wet when we leave and you can walk on it right away.

How much does carpet cleaning cost?

It depends on the number of rooms, the stairs and the condition of the carpet, so we quote each job rather than posting a flat rate. Estimates are free and there's no obligation — call or text (616) 437-0403.

Do you bill insurance for water damage?

Yes. On water and fire loss we document the damage, talk to your adjuster and bill the carrier directly. You're responsible for your deductible and anything your policy doesn't cover.

How often should air ducts be cleaned?

Most homes benefit every three to five years — sooner with pets, allergies, or after remodelling. The best time is before furnace season, since the system then runs for months straight.

Do you offer emergency water damage service?

Yes, 24 hours a day including nights, weekends and holidays. Burst pipes, water heaters, sump failures, ice dams. Call (616) 437-0403 and somebody answers.

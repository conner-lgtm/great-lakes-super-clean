Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

Who we are

Great Lakes Super Clean is a family-owned cleaning and restoration business at 10640 Tallpine Ln, Allendale, MI 49401. You can reach us at (616) 437-0403 or toddglsc@gmail.com.

What we collect

If you fill in the quote form on our website, we collect the name, phone number, town and job details you type in. If you call or text us, we have your phone number. That is all. We do not ask for payment details through this website.

Why we collect it

To call you back and quote your job. That is the only reason.

Who we share it with

  • Nobody, except where a job requires it — for example, sending documentation to your own insurance company on a water or fire loss claim.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not hand it to advertisers or lead brokers.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. The service-area map is embedded from Google Maps, and the fonts are served by Google Fonts; those services may log your IP address as part of delivering them. See Google's own privacy policy for what they do with it.

How long we keep it

Quote requests stay in our email and phone records so we have a history of work done at your property. Ask us to delete yours and we will.

Your choices

Email toddglsc@gmail.com or call (616) 437-0403 to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. If you would rather we did not text you, tell us and we will call instead.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
